Privacy Policy

Qaza Namaz Tracker · Last updated: 6 October 2026

Summary

Qaza Namaz Tracker does not collect, store or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

What the app stores

The app saves your estimate (age, prayers owed), the prayers you count, your pace and your language choice. This is saved only in your browser's local storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app or clear your browser data, it is deleted.

Backups

If you use "Download backup", a file is saved to your device. You decide where that file goes. We never receive it.

No accounts, ads or tracking

There is no sign-up, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking of any kind.

Fonts

To display text in different languages, the app loads fonts from Google Fonts. When fonts are downloaded, your device connects to Google's servers, as with any website using Google Fonts. No information you enter in the app is included in these requests. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
